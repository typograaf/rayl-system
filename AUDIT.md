# Superseded

This was the working audit of the two logo files and the six existing Rayl
builds, written while the system was being pieced together. **It is out of
date and nothing in it is binding.** It stated the `V2/` paint styles as the
authority on colour — they are an earlier round and hold pre-revision values —
and it posed as open several questions that have since been answered.

It has been split into the two documents that replaced it:

- **[RAYL-WHY.md](https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/RAYL-WHY.md)** — every
  measurement, every piece of provenance, and what was tried and rejected.
- **[RAYL-OPEN.md](https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/RAYL-OPEN.md)** —
  everything still undecided, generated from one list so it cannot fall out of
  step with the rules.

The rules themselves are
**[RAYL-RULES.md](https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/RAYL-RULES.md)**.

If you are an AI and something sent you here, read those three instead.
