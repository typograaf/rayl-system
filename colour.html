Rayl / proposal

Colour

Nothing on this page is decided. It is a proposal built from what the existing Rayl files already contain, so that the decision is a yes, a no, or a correction rather than a blank page.

What the existing colours turned out to be

Every colour in Rayl sits on one hue. All ten values measured between 109.5 and 115.6 — the greys at 109.7 with almost no chroma, the green at 115 with the chroma turned up. The greys and the green are the same colour at different intensities.

They are also unevenly spread. Six values crowd between L* 82 and 95, then there is nothing at all until L* 54. That hole is where borders, disabled states and secondary fills live, which is why UI work keeps needing a colour that does not exist.

Proposed ramp — one hue, twelve steps

Denser at the light end, because that is where the interface lives. Every step an existing build already uses is pinned to that build's exact value, so nothing you have shipped has to change. The five marked new fill the hole.

Proposed roles

The point of roles is that nothing ever picks a hex again. It asks for ink / secondary and gets one. When a role is missing, that is a question for you, not a gap to fill with a guess.

Semantic colours — the real decision

Because the whole palette is one hue, a warning or an error is the first hue break in the brand. There is no way around that: nothing made of this hue can mean stop.

These are drawn at the same lightness and chroma as your existing green, so they sit in the same world rather than arriving as web colours. Each has a surface for backgrounds and an ink for text on it.

success #D8DEB9 / #606742 This is your existing green, unchanged. It already sits on the brand hue.
warning #F2D6B7 / #785F40 NEW HUE — the palette has never carried an amber.
error #FFCFCA / #875753 NEW HUE — the palette has never carried a red.
info #BBDFFD / #3D6886 NEW HUE — and the furthest from the brand. Do you even need it?

Worth deciding deliberately: a system this restrained may be better served by one semantic hue rather than four. Error is unavoidable. Success you already have. Warning and info may be doing work that a plain grey and a clear sentence could do instead.

What to rule on

  • Is the ramp right, and are the five new steps the right values?
  • Should surface / page and surface / raised be the same colour? They are today.
  • How many semantic hues — four, two, or one?
  • Is the green success, or is it a brand accent that happens to be green?

Once these are answered they go into RAYL-SYSTEM.md and stop being NOT DECIDED. Back to the system.
