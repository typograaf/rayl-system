Colour

Seven flat colours and two gradients. They live in the Figma file as styles under a V2/ prefix, and those styles are the authority — not this page and not any build.

The names are materials rather than greys, and that is the point. The palette is drawn from the professional catering world: the look of porcelain and concrete, rigid materials given fluid, organic movement.

The hole

Laid out by lightness, the palette is four colours crowded into the top fifth, then nothing at all for 38 points of lightness, then three at the bottom. The hatched band is empty.

That band is where control borders, disabled text and hover states live. It is why UI work keeps reaching for a colour that does not exist, and why every build invented its own.

What to rule on

  • Is there a green? The chapter's own text promises "subtle touches of an organic green" and no green swatch exists. The app build uses #D8DEB9 — same hue as everything else with the chroma raised. In the system, or that screen's invention?
  • Do we fill the hole? One or two mid-tones would end the guessing. They would be new brand colours, so it is your call, not a build's.
  • How many semantic colours? Every colour here is one hue, so anything meaning stop is the first hue break in the brand. Error is the only one that cannot be done another way — success, warning and info may be better served by a grey chip and a clear sentence.
