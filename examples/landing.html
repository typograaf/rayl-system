Rostering for kitchens

Every shift, filled by someone who has done it before.

Rayl matches your open shifts against people who already know the room, the plates and the pace — not a pool of strangers with a star rating.

Written by the people who run the pass

A shift is not a slot in a calendar. It has a section, a station and a person who knows where the ramekins live. Rayl keeps that context attached to the work, so filling a gap is a decision rather than a gamble.

Three things it does that a spreadsheet cannot

01 — Knows the room

Every person carries the stations they have actually worked, not the ones they ticked on a form.

02 — Fills in order

Offers go out to the closest match first and widen only when nobody takes them.

03 — Counts the hours

Overtime and rest are checked as the rota is built, not discovered at the end of the month.

A rota you can read at a glance
