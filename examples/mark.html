The mark in motion

The icon is the solved state.

The mark is already a two-by-two grid, so it is the front face of a two-by-two cube. It scrambles itself, works out the shortest way back, makes the occasional wrong turn, and lands on the mark exactly. Nothing is faked: the turns are real turns and the solution is searched for, not replayed backwards.

What you are watching

Three layers turn — the right column, the top row and the front face. The four quadrants of the icon ride on the four front stickers, so the top bar arrives in two halves and only joins up when every piece is home and the right way up.

The rules it plays by
The pieces

Eight corners. The four facing you carry a quarter of the icon each; the other faces are five steps of the greyscale. The body under the stickers is the ground itself, so a turn never opens a hole in the mark.

The turns

Right, top and front, a quarter or a half at a time. The back-left-bottom corner never moves, so the mark can only ever land square to you and upright.

The solve

A search from both ends at once finds the shortest way home from wherever it is. Sometimes it plays a move that is not on that path, thinks, and finds the way again.
