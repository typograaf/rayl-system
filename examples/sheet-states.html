Every state it has

A ground: a full-frame field that drifts on its own and leans after the pointer, and whose every pixel is between two palette steps. Two gradients, three motions, and one flat state that three different failures all resolve to.

Every colour below comes from a token. The shader emits mix(dark, light, t) clamped to those two, so it cannot produce a colour off the palette even in principle.

Each pair is the same picture. Still is the only state section 7 permits, and four different routes land on it: data-motion="still", a reader who has asked for reduced motion, a browser with no WebGL2, and — once the component's stylesheet is in core.css — a page whose script never ran. Today the fourth route gives a blank box instead, because the stylesheet arrives with the script.

Scale — the shader is in fractions of its own box

96 high

96 high

The ramp across the frame and the ground up it are fractions of the box, so the composition is identical at any size. The cursor disc is measured in pixels over the frame's diagonal divided by root two, which keeps it a circle at every aspect instead of an ellipse that reshapes itself when the window is dragged.

The state that is not shown here, and why 
<div data-rayl-sheet="concrete" data-place="page"></div>

place="page" puts the sheet behind the whole page on position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:-1, and it needs the grounds on body.rayl and rayl-page cleared by hand to be seen at all. That is the layering item in RAYL-OPEN.md — the system has no layering vocabulary, and every z-index in core.css is inside a component. It is not demonstrated on this page because demonstrating it means taking the page over. The component marks any element using it data-rayl-provisional itself.
