The Rayl design system

The rules for how anything Rayl looks. Hand this to an AI and whatever it builds — a new tool, a change to the app, a reskin, a screensaver — comes out looking like Rayl, including the parts nobody has designed yet.

You do not need to read any code to use it.

How to use it

Copy the block below and paste it at the top of your prompt, before whatever you are asking for. That is the whole workflow.

Build this to the Rayl design system.

Read https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/RAYL-SYSTEM.md first and follow it exactly. Read https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/AUDIT.md too if you need to know why something is the way it is.

The rules that matter most:

- The logo is a file, never something you draw. Icon: https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/assets/logo/Icon.svg  Lockup: https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/assets/logo/Lockup.svg  Use the lockup where there is room, the icon where there is not.
- The typeface is Azeret: https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/assets/fonts/Azeret-TRIAL-Regular.woff2 and https://typograaf.github.io/rayl-system/assets/fonts/Azeret-TRIAL-Medium.woff2
- Text is trimmed to its cap height, not its line box. In CSS that is text-box-trim: trim-both and text-box-edge: cap alphabetic. Skip this and every measurement will be wrong.
- Spacing is 6, 12, 24, 48, 72 and nothing else.
- Type is 8 for section labels and 12 for everything else, tracking 0.02em, line height 1.2.
- Ink is #1C1C1A. Light mode only.
- Where the system does not cover something, tell me and ask. Do not invent it and do not substitute something that looks close.

Where this is up to

The logo section is complete and measured off the real files. Colour, type sizes above 12, component states, motion, icons and the card look are all still open. They are written up as numbered questions in the audit.

Anything marked NOT DECIDED is a genuine gap. The document tells the AI to stop and ask when it hits one rather than filling it in with something plausible — a guess that looks fine is the thing that quietly becomes the new standard.

Two things this page is proving

This page is built to the system's own rules and no others, so its limits are the system's limits.

It has no heading size, because no size above 12 has been agreed. It is set at a line height of 1.2, which is right for a control and tight for a paragraph. Both of those are answered by question 7 in the audit.

The background is #F0F0E5, chosen provisionally from the tool panel. Question 2 settles it properly.
